Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how EasySnake (Android package com.w3stech.easysnake, operated by W3S Tech) handles information when you use the app.

1. Information we collect

EasySnake is designed to keep data collection minimal.

  • Game preferences on device: settings such as difficulty, sound, haptics, selected skin, high score, and unlocked stickers are stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads via Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect device and usage information (for example advertising ID, IP address, device type, and ad interaction data) to serve and measure ads according to their policies.
  • We do not require you to create an account, and we do not intentionally collect names, email addresses, or payment card details inside the app.

2. How information is used

  • To run gameplay features and remember your local preferences.
  • To show ads and support the free version of the app through AdMob.
  • To improve stability and performance where crash or diagnostic data is provided by the platform or ad SDKs.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

EasySnake uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Ad serving is handled by Google. For details on how Google uses data, see Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob & privacy.

You can learn about ad personalization controls in your device settings (for example Reset advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization on Android) and via Google Ads Settings.

4. Children’s privacy

EasySnake is a casual game. If you believe we have collected personal information from a child in a way that is not permitted, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it. Ad partners may apply their own child-directed or age-related policies when serving ads.

5. Data retention & security

Local game data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. Advertising data is retained by Google according to their policies. We use reasonable technical measures appropriate for a mobile app, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Third-party services

The app may rely on:

  • Google Play services
  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK

Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

7. Your choices

  • Uninstall the app to remove local game data from your device.
  • Adjust device ad and privacy settings as available on your Android version.
  • Review Google’s privacy tools linked above for advertising preferences.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact
